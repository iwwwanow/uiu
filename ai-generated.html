ai-generated

not hand-crafted

These components were generated by AI from the conventions of owo and were not processed by hand. Every class carries the _ai-generated suffix, so it never collides with owo and is easy to find.

Rules borrowed from owo: the h4 / h5 / h6 / p type scale, 1px LIGHT borders that turn DARK on hover, BLUE for anything interactive, RED for danger, no radius (except radio and spinner), no transitions, no uppercase.
